    <h1>All About MRI Technology</h1>

    <h2>What Is an MRI?</h2>

    <p>
        MRI stands for Magnetic Resonance Imaging. It is a type
        of medical imaging that uses a strong magnetic field and
        radio waves to create detailed pictures of the inside
        of the body. MRI provides great tissue contrast that is important for doctors to visualize different pathologies in organs, bones, muscles, and ligaments. 
    </p>

    <p>
        One thing I find interesting about MRI is that unlike other modaliteies such as X-ray,Cat scan, and mammography  it can
        produce detailed images without using <strong>ionizing
        radiation</strong>. Which makes it safer for patients, but also calls for a very intense screening process.
    </p>

    <h2>Life as an MRI Student</h2>

    <p>
        I am currently a student in the Radiologic Technology major and my concentration is MRI. Every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday I go to UCCON Hospital and work with MRI Techs as I prepare for my MRI boards. I get to screen patients, set then up for their scan, and scan different parts of anatomy. It is very eye opening seeing how the techs deal with patients that may have claustrophobia or may be emootional due to being scared for their diagnosis. As a tech it is important to keep calm and do what is best for you and your patient.
